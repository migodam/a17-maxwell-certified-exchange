## Strongest findings

1. Four geometries and Gaussian/voxel spaces show substantial same-dimension complete-step improvement with at most three checked exchanges.
2. Same-checkpoint scoring and coverage are quantitatively separated. Accurate full-dictionary ranking coexists with poor short-pool coverage.
3. A saved Maxwell replacement increases information volume by 6.3127 and effective dimension by 1.8613, yet gives a gain of −5.5754e−5. Measured curvature fidelity also improves slightly. Actual displacement depends jointly on offset and curvature.
4. Two negative singleton additions combine into a positive joint addition on a common Maxwell core.

## Paper positioning

Mechanism + conditional current-design paper. Lead with electromagnetic entry/feedback/return, paired material support, actual finite displacement and measured same-budget improvement. Schur elimination, Woodbury, quadratic identities and information summaries are established algebra. No claim of being first-ever, of native TSOM superiority, universal ranking, a strict certificate, or faster reconstruction.

The broad A16 FAIL_KEEP_MECHANISM remains unchanged. The new exchange dictionary/seed, receiver start, conditional rescoring and physical checks answer a narrower local question.
