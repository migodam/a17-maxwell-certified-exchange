## Claims that can be stated directly

1. A specified Maxwell current modification has state-dependent material injection and a feedback-dressed receiver return; its normal equations expose both curvature and residual-dependent changes.
2. The finite signed gain of the actual material displacement supplies a concrete conditional design decision on a common full quadratic.
3. At fixed current dimension, the frozen verified-exchange rule substantially improves full-GN step fidelity in the reported development and additional-object tests.
4. Candidate acquisition and multistep interactions are distinct from local reduced ranking accuracy. The current short pool leaves measurable opportunity uncovered.

The work does not establish a universal ranking, native-TSOM superiority, deterministic output certificate, acceleration, or broadly improved final imaging. Each of the three complete reconstruction pairs takes more all-in child wall time than its receiver-only counterpart.
