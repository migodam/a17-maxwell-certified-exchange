# Technical review context

The review target is the manuscript and evidence in this `closure_final/` directory. The root-level historical package is retained for provenance and should not be substituted for this final manuscript.

The strongest result is an electromagnetic model-design result: a locked, receiver-centered conditional exchange improves full-GN material-step fidelity at fixed current rank across the reported additional objects. The mechanism follows material injection, retained multiple-scattering feedback and receiver return, then evaluates the finite material displacement generated by a particular replacement.

For review, the most useful checks are:

- Does the argument distinguish Maxwell-specific transfer from established Schur, least-squares, goal-oriented ROM and high-fidelity acceptance tools?
- Is TSOM credited for internal propagation, and are proxy banks distinguished from native algorithms?
- Are the object-level statistics, absolute gaps, incomplete states and historical exposure stated accurately?
- Does the local GN-gap endpoint remain distinct from final material error and nonlinear acceptance?
- Are same current rank, additional information and complete computational cost kept separate?
- Are candidate coverage, reduced scoring error and bounded path effects separated?
- Does the numerical tolerance remain an online significance criterion, without being promoted to a deterministic output certificate?

Important counterevidence remains visible. Broad from-scratch material-aware ranking was not generally superior. Earlier paired material repair lost to equal-rank material Krylov and was slower after full payment. The present nonlinear pairs cost more, the near-contact real error increases slightly, and the fourth pair is incomplete. None of these facts is overwritten by the positive frozen-update evidence.

The reference audit identifies acquired primary full texts and open original-paper gaps. Submission readiness is assessed separately from evidence-package completion; no acceptance prediction or historical-priority claim follows from hashes, tests or successful publication.
